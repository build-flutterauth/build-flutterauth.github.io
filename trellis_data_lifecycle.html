Review-Ready — pre-live holding stage for collected and confirmed inputs. Little validation on entry; editable; joinable with other datasets. A human validates to promote.
Trusted-Source — pre-live capture for Metrc and other system-validated imports. No intervention required; auto-flows to Live. Captured as its own state so transition webhooks fire even for trusted data.
Live — validated and eligible for transactions, dashboards, and order flow.
Historic — the license hinge: when a license expires, every record scoped to it leaves Live automatically. Still present on historic reports; closed for transactions; restored if the license renews.
Source methods: collected — provided by the MSO · derived — synced from Metrc / trusted systems · confirmed — exists in two systems, a human joins and attests · generated — produced by Trellis.
